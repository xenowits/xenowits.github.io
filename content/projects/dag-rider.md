An implementation of [DAG-Rider](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2102.08325.pdf) in go. DAG-Rider is the first asynchronous Byzantine Atomic Broadcast protocol that achieves optimal resilience, optimal amortized communication complexity, and optimal time complexity.
